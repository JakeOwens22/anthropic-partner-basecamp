### 4. Fare families

**Basic.** Our lowest fare. No voluntary changes and no value retained if you cancel. Seats are assigned at the gate. When *we* disrupt your trip (section 5), Basic is treated exactly like Main: every waiver below applies.

**Main.** Changes permitted with no change fee; any fare difference applies and is collected by Customer Care, not in chat. Non-refundable: a voluntary cancellation returns the ticket value as a Larkspur travel credit valid 12 months.

**Main Plus.** As Main, plus: refundable to the original form of payment, and one free same-day confirmed change to another Larkspur flight between the same cities on the same calendar day when a Main-cabin seat is open.

**First.** Refundable, free same-day confirmed change when a First seat is open. If a disruption leaves only Main-cabin seats, you may take one and Customer Care will refund the cabin difference.

Skyline tier does not change fare rules. It changes care and courtesies (section 6).

### 5. When we delay, cancel or divert your flight

We classify the cause of every disruption. **Within our control:** aircraft maintenance, crew availability. **Outside our control:** weather, air traffic control restrictions, security events at the airport. Agents name the cause using those labels only.

**Under 60 minutes.** Your ticket is unchanged. Main Plus and First customers may still use their free same-day change.

**60 minutes or more, any cause, any fare including Basic.** You may move to another Larkspur or Larkspur Link flight between the same cities at no cost, in the same cabin, for travel up to 1 day earlier or up to 3 days later (outside our control) or 7 days later (within our control). Everyone on the booking moves together; splitting a party needs Customer Care. We do not rebook onto other airlines through chat; stations and the IRROPS desk make that call.

**Cancellations, diversions, and delays of 3 hours or more.** In addition to free rebooking, if you choose not to travel you may request a refund of the unused ticket to the original form of payment, or a travel credit, whichever you prefer. If a cancelled outbound defeats the purpose of a same-booking return, the return is refundable too. Refunds are processed by Customer Care, never by the chat assistant; card refunds are typically visible within 7 business days.

**Diversions.** If we land somewhere other than your destination, we get you to your ticketed destination by air or, when the diversion airport is within 150 road miles, by arranged ground transport. An individual $40 ground transport credit applies only when no airline transport leaves within 3 hours.

### 6. Care while you wait (per passenger)

| Situation | Within our control | Outside our control |
|---|---|---|
| Delay 2 to 3 hours | $15 meal credit | none |
| Delay 3 hours or more | $25 meal credit | $15 meal credit |
| Cancelled or diverted, waiting for your new flight | $25 meal credit after 2 hours | $15 meal credit after 3 hours |
| Overnight away from your home airport | Hotel up to $175 (1 night, approved by an agent) plus $40 ground transport | Not covered; we share a distressed-traveler hotel rate |

Gold and Summit members receive $10 more on any meal credit, up to $25. Summit members stranded overnight by weather or ATC may be offered a courtesy hotel at an agent's discretion.

**Goodwill.** For long delays (3 hours or more), cancellations and diversions *within our control*, agents may offer each passenger a future travel credit as a goodwill gesture, once per disruption: up to $25 (Member), $50 (Silver), $100 (Gold), $150 (Summit), plus $25 on Main Plus and $50 on First fares, and a further $25 when the delay exceeds 6 hours. Basic fares qualify from Silver upward. Goodwill is discretionary and is not an admission that anything is owed. For causes outside our control there is no goodwill, except a Summit courtesy of up to $50 that always needs a person to approve. Credits up to $75 may be issued directly; $76 to $200 need an agent's approval; above $200 needs a supervisor.

### 7. What chat automation will not do

Refunds, fare-difference collection, hotel approval, other-airline rebooking, group bookings of 10 or more, unaccompanied minors, itineraries with partner-operated flights, pets in cabin and medical requests on a new flight, and baggage tracing are handled by people. The assistant explains, holds seats for 15 minutes while you decide, and hands over with a summary. Nothing is rebooked until you press confirm.
